# Leg day (LegDay): analysis and edit

## Original
46.0 s, 720×1280 (60 fps container, ~30 unique fps), no dialogue. "GYM VLOG / Leg Day" title over the intro, 8
exercises with a label at the very bottom (y 0.88–0.97, inside the Reels UI zone) reading "<exercise> 3x15" (one typo:
"Sated calf raises"), flashes at 11.87, 13.43, 22.30, 37.27, 44.93. Soundtrack: a commercial rap track with explicit lyrics.
Strength: good variety of angles. Weakness: 46 s of mostly real-time sets, labels in the unsafe zone, a soundtrack the
brand can't use.

## New edit (29.5 s + 2.4 s end card)
Beat-cut montage at 120 bpm: intro + 8 exercises of ~3.3 s each; whip / zoom-flash between exercises, hard angle cuts
with settle + slam inside them, 2× speed ramps on the three machines, one freeze frame (deadlift lockout) with a
DEADLIFT title, boom and camera shake. Hook LEG DAY + "8 EXERCISES, 3 × 15". Each exercise gets a lower label (name +
3 × 15 counting up) in the safe area; the old labels sit under a cinematic bottom fade. "Sated" is corrected to SEATED.
Music: A minor drive with builds and stops landing on the first exercise, the freeze and the leg-extension ramp.
