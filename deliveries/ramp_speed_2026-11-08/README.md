## Video 1: RAMP warm-up
1. **Hook:** a box jump that ramps into 120 fps slow motion, then your question "قبل ما تبدأ تجري أو تتمرن، هل بتجهز جسمك صح؟"
2. **RAMP reveal:** the letters R·A·M·P land one by one on the beat while the four phases flash.
3. **The four phases**, each with a phase card and its minutes:
   - RAISE (3–4 MIN), with your jogging;
   - ACTIVATE (2–3 MIN), with single-leg work and hinges, plus a "ONE-LEG SQUAT" callout;
   - MOBILIZE (5–6 MIN), with leg swings and a "90/90" callout;
   - POTENTIATE (3–4 MIN), with "20 M SPRINTS", 70% / 80% / 90% hits on your spoken numbers, and box jumps for "POGOS" and "BROAD JUMP".
   A RAMP phase bar under the captions fills as you go.
4. **"15 MIN" total**, then "BETTER PERFORMANCE", "FEWER INJURIES" and the OSM end card.

## Video 2: 3 things to get faster
1. **Hook:** you running at the lens, ramping into slow motion, with "RUNNING ISN'T ENOUGH" and "3 THINGS".
2. **01 ACCELERATION:** a live speed-over-time graph draws the acceleration curve.
3. **02 MAX VELOCITY:** the curve reaches its plateau.
4. **03 RUNNING MECHANICS, "the most important":** the graph highlights acceleration, then max velocity, as you say each one.
5. **Close:** "RUN RIGHT." and "FOLLOW FOR MORE", where you say each topic gets its own video.

## Pace, music and sound
- **Fast cutting:** pauses and dead air are removed. The picture changes about every 1–1.5 s, alternating close and wide framing and B-roll cutaways under your voice.
- **Speed ramps:** these use the real 120 fps footage, not frame interpolation.
- **Music:** an original track made for each video (Video 1 trap, Video 2 phonk, both 120 BPM; no licence issues). Every chapter starts exactly on the beat, and the drops land on the RAMP reveal and on "running mechanics".
- **Mix:** the music ducks under your voice. Measured: it stays at least 12 dB under you in about 90% of speech, and comes up between your lines.
- **Sound effects:** synthesised in-house, matched to every cut, title hit, the counter ticks and the end card.
- **Want it without the music?** I can export versions with voice and sound effects only, so you can use an Instagram song.

## Brand rules applied
- **Captions:** locked OSM caption bar, #56A0FB with white IBM Plex Sans Arabic, your exact words, English terms in English.
- **Your face:** no text on or above your face. Every title is placed using face detection, and the final videos passed the automatic head check.
- **Safe area and effects:** nothing below the bottom 14% safe area, no blur or glow behind text.
- **Footage:** no AI footage and no stock. Everything is your own footage, from this shoot plus your B-roll library (the box-jump clip).
