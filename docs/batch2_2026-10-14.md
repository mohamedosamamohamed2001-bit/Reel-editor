## Limitations (stated honestly)
- **No external video clips.** The free stock-video sites (Pexels, Pixabay) need API keys or block this environment,
  and Wikimedia rate-limited downloads. Right/wrong technique is shown with his own demo footage labelled ✓ / ✗, and
  anatomy with public-domain plates; no licensed exercise clips were added.
- Anatomy markers sit on the named muscle regions of 19th-century plates; individual triceps heads are not labelled
  on the plate itself (only TRICEPS / LONG HEAD / HUMERUS / SCAPULA), to avoid mislabelling.
- The sources are finished 720p edits with burned-in captions. Since v2 (no blur) the old white captions show behind
  and next to the new blue-bar captions. Raw clips without captions would give a clean result.
- Captions use his original caption wording aligned to the voice; a few words were added where he clearly says them
  and the old caption left them out ("الحقيقة", "ليك انت"); "Prime move" is written as the correct term "Prime mover".
  These, and the audio itself, need a by-ear check.
