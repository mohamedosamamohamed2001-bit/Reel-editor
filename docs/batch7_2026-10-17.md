## Studio changes
- `tools/erase_plates.py`:
  - `--neck`: the face guard's reach below the chin. Selfies use 0.12, because the old plates sit on the chest.
  - `--face-score`: a blurred hand scored 0.7 as a "face" and blocked the erase.
  - `--matched`: for handheld shots. Each frame's plate area comes from ONE nearby frame, aligned by phase correlation on a ring around the plate, and only if the ring then matches. Otherwise Telea inpainting is used, with a feathered paste.
  - Why: nearest-pixel patchwork pasted skin over the shirt when a hand passed.
  - Long chunks must be ≤ ~300 frames at 1080×1920: a 550-frame chunk was OOM-killed.
- `projects/_tips/common4.py` `Tip(end=…)`: exercise demos keep their silent reps after the last word.
- **`projects/_b7/b7.py` `lock(P, blue_shirt=True)`:**
  - His blue shirt is close to Core Blue, so blue accent lines and the small blue eyebrow vanish into it. Titles over the footage are therefore white (3D depth kept).
  - Labels and step badges sit on the solid plate.
  - Full-screen cards keep the accent.
- `projects/_b7/b7.py` `image()`: openly licensed photos from `projects/_b7/assets` (manifest, CC BY credit on screen).
