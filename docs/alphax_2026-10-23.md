## Why the songs are not inside the files
These are commercial recordings. No licence for them is available here, and embedding them would be ripping copyrighted audio.

What the files contain instead:
- Every cut lands on that song's beat grid.
- The only audio is the original camera sound removed and replaced with an SFX bed (whooshes, impacts, risers), synthesised in-house with no samples.
- The song is added through Instagram/TikTok's music library, which licenses it for the post.

## Trend research and its limits
Instagram and TikTok can't be opened from here: Instagram returned HTTP 429. The picks therefore come from third-party trend trackers dated October 2026, not from the apps' own charts:
- Timber: SocialBee and DashSocial. Back in circulation for fast sports and fitness edits.
- Pray For Me: Newengen's October TikTok trends.
- Anything Could Happen: DashSocial. Used for fitness and goal Reels. This one is listed under March 2026, so it is the oldest pick.
- Sweet But Psycho: TikTok "trending gym songs" discover page.

Tempos come from published BPM listings:
- Timber: 130.
- Pray For Me: 101.
- Anything Could Happen: 103 (album version, unconfirmed by a primary source).
- Sweet But Psycho: 133 (Beatport original mix).
