## How they were made (`projects/_r16/reel.py`)
- **Voice:** his whole take, unbroken and in real time. Nothing he says is cut.
- **Reframing:** YuNet face positions (x 0.47–0.53 across all takes) give a centred 9:16 crop from the 4K frame, with slow pushes and punch-ins (e.g. onto the supplements on his table).
- **Captions:** his exact words. The large-v3 and medium transcripts were compared, then the checked text was aligned to the word timings (`analysis/reference.txt`).
  - Bars are **white**, per his 2026-10-16 brief: Night text, one Core Blue keyword, karaoke word timing, no blur or haze.
  - English terms stay English. "الـApp" style words render Arabic prefix + isolated English part.
- **Cutaways:** all placed while he keeps talking:
  - the new screen recording: today's challenge, the 420-point balance, the rewards (C4 preworkout / EAA / creatine at 7,000 pts) and the points history (+300, +100, +10);
  - the earlier demo-account recordings: coach chat, workout plan, progress, home (the account name is cropped out);

  - two Apple Watch photos (Flickr, CC BY 2.0, credited on screen, `projects/_r16/assets/manifest.json`);
  - Coverr AirPods footage (free, non-AI).
- **Graphics:** icon tiles, labels and titles. `tools/head_check.py` is clean on all three. Where a graphic could not fit between his chin and the captions, the beat moves to a clean brand card (r2 "3 فايزين بس", r3 "اكسب أكتر").
