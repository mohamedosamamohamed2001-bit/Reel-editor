## What's new in the system
- `tools/erase_text.py` v2: contrast + colour glyph rules, stroke density, per-colour shape tests, colour-only windows,
  static-prop guard, **face guard** (YuNet landmarks: face + beard are never erased), resolution scaling.
- Behind-the-head titles: `tools/cutout.py` (MODNet matte) + `kineticTitle.occlude`.
- `IconPop` illustration tiles (lucide-react icons, ISC licence) with draw-on strokes, orbit ring, markers.
- `projects/_b3/common3.py` `Story`: transitions on every old cut, clean held frames over the old edit's flashes /
  light leaks / glitches, SFX per element, music sections built from the cuts.
- Licensed images: `projects/_b3/assets/manifest.json` (Openverse; CC0 / public domain first, CC BY credited on screen).

## Where old text could not be erased (and what was done instead)

- Dark-red caption words on his red shirt (pre-workout 8.5-11.6 s, 14.9-16.6 s) and text merged with the printed box
